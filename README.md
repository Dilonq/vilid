# vilid

## Ideologies

### Positions

There are 5 positions ranging from Far Left, Left, Moderate, Right, and Far Right.

### Ideology List

Far Left - Communist, Anarchist

Left - Progressive, Green

Moderate - Moderate

Right - Libertarian, Conservative

Far Right - Reactionary, Fascist

## Commands

### Utility

#### /rename <name>

Renames itemstack in main hand.

#### /elect <nation name> <party name>

Elects a party to power in a specific nation.

### Registration

#### /registernation <name>

Registers a nation.

#### /registerparty <nation name> <party name> <ideology>

Registers a party with a specified ideology in a specified nation.

#### /unregisternation <name>

Unregisters a nation from the game. Unregisters all parties affiliated with it.

#### /unregisterparty <name>

Unregisters a party from the game.

### Polling

#### /poll <nation name>

Lists all ideologies and how many villager adherents from the specific nation.

#### /medianhappiness <nation name>

Displays the median happiness value of every villager in a specific nation.

#### /getrulingparty <nation name>

Displays the ruling party of the specific nation.

#### /listideologies

Lists all ideologies and how many villager adherents they have.

#### /listnations

Lists all nations.

#### /listparties <nation>

Lists all parties within a nation.
